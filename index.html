Frequently Asked Questions

How do Social Security and a 401(k) work together in retirement?

Social Security provides guaranteed, inflation-adjusted baseline income, while your 401(k) offers discretionary investment growth and variable withdrawals to bridge retirement living expenses.

How does claiming age affect my monthly Social Security benefits?

Claiming at age 62 permanently reduces your monthly benefit by up to 30%, whereas delaying until age 70 increases your monthly benefit by 8% per year past Full Retirement Age.

What is the 4% safe withdrawal rule for 401(k) accounts?

The 4% rule is an empirical financial guideline suggesting retirees can withdraw 4% of their initial portfolio balance in the first year, adjusting for inflation annually, with high probability of lasting 30 years.
